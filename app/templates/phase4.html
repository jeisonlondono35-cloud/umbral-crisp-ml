Phase 4

Model Evaluation

Which model is best, measured the same way for all of them, checked for over-fitting and bias, reviewed by the domain expert, and finally measured once on players that were never used to choose or tune anything.

4.1 Metrics and why

TaskMain metric (selection)Also reportedWhy
Next actionAccuracyMacro-F1, log lossThe boss acts on the single most likely action, so "how often it is right" is the business number (the game shows it to players). Classes are unbalanced → macro-F1. The boss also weighs the probability → log loss.
HitBrier scoreROC-AUC, log lossThe boss picks the attack with the highest probability: the probabilities must be calibrated (Brier), and rank well (AUC).
DodgeMean Euclidean error (px)-Directly the miss distance of the boss aim.

4.2 Validation adapted to the structure of the data

The data are sequences grouped by player, so random K-fold would put the same player in training and validation and overestimate the score. We use GroupKFold(5) by player for the hyper-parameters, a separate set of validation players to choose, and prequential (predict-then-learn) evaluation for the online models, which is how they work in the game.
