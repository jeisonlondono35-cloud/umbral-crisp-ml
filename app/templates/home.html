UMBRAL is a pixel-art action game (Godot 4, published on the web, Android and PC) made by J.A · J.P · J.Q. Its bosses adapt to the person playing: while you fight, small models learn what you usually do and the boss reacts (it feints if it expects your shield, waits if it expects your dodge, aims where you usually go). This project takes that machine learning out of the game, studies it with real and simulated play data, compares it with other algorithms, chooses the best model with a criterion fixed in advance, deploys it behind this Flask app and plans how to monitor it.

Activity 2 · The UMBRAL player information system

The model is now used inside the business processes of the game's players and accounts: player profiles and saved games, play sessions, records and support. When a play session arrives, an analyst runs the model on it, the model proposes a fair difficulty for that player, a person approves or rejects it, the account changes and the next session shows if the change worked. Every inference is stored with its user, model version and response time.

Player

Creates an account, manages only their own profile, saved games, sessions, records and tickets, and sees what the model learned about them.

Analyst

Runs the model on sessions, approves or rejects difficulty changes, verifies records, manages tickets, reports and monitoring.

Administrator

Everything an analyst does, plus users, roles and the audit log.
